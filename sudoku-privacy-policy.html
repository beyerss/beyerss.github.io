Privacy Policy for Sudoku: Notes & Colors

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Sudoku: Notes & Colors ("Sudoku", "we", "our", or "us") handles your information when you use our app on iPhone, iPad, Mac or Android (the "Service").

Information We Collect

We don't collect any personal data. There's no account, no analytics, no tracking and no ads.

Data Stored Locally

The app saves the following only on your device:

  • Games: puzzles in progress, with your notes, colors and undo history
  • Statistics: solve times, streaks and daily challenge results
  • Settings: your preferences, such as theme, highlighting and hints

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identification information
  • Device identifiers
  • Location data
  • Contact information
  • Photos or media files
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to us

Purchases

Sudoku Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription. It is bought and managed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) through your account with them. The store tells the app whether you have an active subscription; we never see your name, email address or payment details.

Leaderboards and Achievements

If you're signed in to Game Center (Apple) or Google Play Games (Google), your daily solve times and achievements are sent to that service under your account with them, and other players may see your scores. The app sends nothing else, and we never see this data. You can turn this off in Game Center settings on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, or in the Google Play Games app.

Ratings

The app may ask you to rate it. Ratings and reviews are handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play, and the app doesn't see what you choose.

Data Storage and Security

  • Local Storage: your games, settings and stats stay on your device
  • No External Transmission: we do not transmit, share, or upload any of your data to our own or anyone else's servers, except the solve times and achievements described under Leaderboards and Achievements
  • No Analytics: we do not use any analytics services or tracking tools
  • No Third-Party Services: apart from the App Store or Google Play for purchases and ratings, and Game Center or Google Play Games for leaderboards and achievements, the app uses no third-party services and works offline

Data Retention and Deletion

  • Retention: data is kept on your device until you delete it
  • Deletion: deleting or uninstalling the app removes all of its data. On Android you can also use "Clear storage" in the app's system settings.
  • Subscriptions: deleting the app doesn't cancel Sudoku Pro. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

Children's Privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
